# Returns Policy

Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, in original packaging, and accompanied by the order number. Returns requested after 30 days are reviewed case by case and may be offered store credit instead of a refund.

Electronics have a shorter 15-day return window because of rapid depreciation. Opened electronics are accepted only if defective, and a technician inspection is required before the refund is approved.

Clothing and footwear can be exchanged for a different size free of charge within 30 days. Innerwear, swimwear and personalised items cannot be returned for hygiene reasons unless they arrive damaged.

Return shipping is free when the item is defective or we sent the wrong product. In all other cases a flat return fee of ₹99 is deducted from the refund amount.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the warehouse receives and inspects the item. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to the customer's ShopPilot wallet or a verified bank account.

Damaged-in-transit claims must be raised within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the package and the product. Claims raised later than 48 hours require manager approval.
